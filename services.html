FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got straight, transparent answers. No marketing fluff.

How do you choose your micro-creators? +
We screen creators based on real engagement parameters, not just follower counts. We look for active comment sections, high watch-time metrics, regional dialect affinity, and how authentic their relationship is with their audience.
What is your typical campaign turnaround time? +
A regional campaign typically takes 2–4 weeks from initial brief to going live. This includes creator sourcing, onboarding, content scripting, local shooting, alignment/approvals, and scheduling the publish dates.
Do you handle creator contracts and payouts? +
Yes, we provide end-to-end management. We draft clear briefs, manage individual content contracts, track deliverable timelines, and handle payouts so your marketing team only deals with one single agency invoice.
How do you track conversion data for apps or brands? +
We integrate unique trackable links, discount/promo codes, and custom landing pages for each creator. In addition to views/likes, we provide a campaign performance dashboard mapping exact direct conversions, app installs, and lead queries.
