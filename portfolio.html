Case Study

Space Seven Fitness

How We Helped Space Seven Fitness Turn Views into Foot Traffic

+

The Challenge: Cutting Through the Noise

Mohali has a thriving fitness scene, but that also means the market is incredibly loud. Space Seven Fitness isn't just another gym—it’s a premium training facility with top-tier equipment and an intense, supportive community. The problem? Generic "gym mirror selfies" weren't going to convey that vibe.

We needed a way to cut through the noise and show people exactly what it feels like to train there, moving the needle from passive scrolling to actual walk-ins.

Our Approach: Real Sweat, Real Results

Instead of just paying influencers to show up, we wanted to build a campaign around authenticity.

  • Quality Over Quantity: We meticulously scouted and successfully onboarded 8 local creators who genuinely aligned with the gym's hard-working ethos. It was a conscious choice to prioritize authentic content that perfectly showcased the premium facility.
  • Ditching the Generic Script: We didn't want standard promotional videos. Our team sat down and drafted highly technical, workout-specific briefs for each creator. We mapped out exactly which machines to highlight, what training forms to focus on, and how to capture the true scale of the facility. We wanted the audience to learn something, not just look at something.
  • Boots on the Ground: You can't capture the energy of a gym from behind a laptop. We were on-site at Space Seven Fitness for the shoots, coordinating directly with the creators. Being there in person allowed us to direct the lighting, set the pacing, and make sure the premium feel of the space translated perfectly on camera. It was hands-on, high-energy, and ensured that every piece of content met the standard we promised.
